      About / method

      This page puts the 16 individual suburb maps together. It reuses the suburbs' own overlay images and address files and loads them only when needed: overlays load for suburbs in view from zoom 12, address scores (click-for-scores and the address dots) from zoom 15, and the dots show from zoom 16. The address search loads a small combined index the first time you use it.

      Each overlay is clipped to its own suburb boundary (LINZ NZ Suburbs and Localities), so neighbouring suburbs meet at the boundary line instead of overlapping. The individual pages extend 100 m past the boundary. Areas outside these 16 suburbs, or more than 400 m from an address, are blank.

      Sun and shade come from a horizon-angle model run on the LINZ Wellington City 2025 1 m LiDAR DSM, gap-filled with the 2013–14 survey, plus an 8 m DSM out to 12 km for distant hills. The sun's position is sampled every 10 minutes for all of 2026 (pvlib SPA). Cloud isn't modelled. Morning = sunrise to solar noon, Afternoon = solar noon to sunset. Garden = 1.7 m above ground at the LINZ address point; Roof = top of the matched LINZ building outline.
