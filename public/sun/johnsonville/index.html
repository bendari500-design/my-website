About / method

Sun and shade come from a horizon-angle model run on the LINZ Wellington City 2025 1 m LiDAR DSM, gap-filled with the 2013–14 survey, plus an 8 m DSM out to 12 km for distant hills. The sun's position is sampled every 10 minutes for all of 2026 (pvlib SPA). Cloud isn't modelled.

Morning = sunrise to solar noon, Afternoon = solar noon to sunset. Garden (headline) = 1.7 m above ground at the LINZ address point, ignoring the house itself. Roof = top of the matched LINZ building outline. The map overlay shows the top surface (roofs, tree canopy, open ground) at 2 m, clipped to the Johnsonville suburb boundary (+100 m) and to within 400 m of an address; areas with no LiDAR data (e.g. sea) are left blank.
